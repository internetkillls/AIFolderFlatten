# Research Intelligence

Research output is heuristic and candidate-oriented. It does not browse,
resolve DOIs, judge novelty, or claim academic validity.

Current pack files:

- `RESEARCH/RESEARCH_SIGNAL_REPORT.md`
- `RESEARCH/TERM_BANK.md`
- `RESEARCH/METHOD_PHRASE_MAP.md`
- `RESEARCH/GAP_QUESTIONS.md`
- `RESEARCH/PROVENANCE_HINTS.md`

Signals come from deterministic n-grams and phrase matching. Gap questions are
handoff prompts for later human or external research, not discovered facts.
